    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about bidding on BIDURS.

    You can register for a free account by clicking the "Register" button in the top right corner. You'll need to provide your full name, email, and mobile number, and complete an OTP verification.

    A subscription is required to participate in eligible auctions. Once purchased from your Dashboard, the subscription validity is strictly 1 month (30 days) from the activation date.

    Navigate to any Live Auction page. You can use the plus/minus buttons to adjust your bid above the minimum required increment. The highest bidder is the user with the highest recorded bid when the auction countdown timer reaches exactly zero.

    If you win, the auction moves to your "Won Auctions" tab. A strict payment deadline timer will begin. You must complete full payment before this deadline expires to secure the product.

    If the highest bidder fails to pay within the deadline, they lose their right to the product. The company may offer it to the next eligible bidder or cancel/reschedule the auction entirely.

    In the event of technical issues, the company may stop the auction when necessary. The auction may be rescheduled if interrupted. The company reserves the right to make the final decision.
